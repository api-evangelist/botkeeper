Let’s be honest for a second: how many times have you and your team spent the first week and a half of a new month buried under mountains of receipts, frantic bank reconciliations, and endless spreadsheet cross-checking? If you are running an accounting firm or managing Client Accounting Services (CAS), the month-end close is likely one of your biggest operational bottlenecks. Long story short, it drains your team's energy, delays critical client insights, and caps the number of clients your firm can take on.
